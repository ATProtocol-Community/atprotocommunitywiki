# ATProto Protocol Working Session at IETF124

[IETF124 takes place in Montreal, Nov 1st - 7th](https://www.ietf.org/meeting/124/), and we are doing some early planning about gathering together a Protocol Working Session -- bringing in people who are working on AT Protocol and implementing and proposing changes to it.

The likely topics include OAuth Scopes, E2EE Messaging, and Private Data, but anyone doing protocol work is welcome to attend and contribute.
